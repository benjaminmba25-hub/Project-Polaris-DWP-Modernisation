## 1. Executive Summary
**Project Polaris** is a simulated functional portfolio designed to demonstrate the end-to-end lifecycle of a DWP-style digital transformation project.

The objective of this simulation is to showcase the modernisation of a "Legacy Mainframe" claims processing system into a cloud-native, user-centred web platform. It specifically addresses the challenges of **interoperability**, **data migration**, and **user experience (UX)** for both Citizens and Case Managers.

This repository serves as evidence of my capability to deliver **Requirements Analysis**, **Data Modelling**, and **Process Optimisation** within complex multi-project environments.

---

## 2. Domain Knowledge & Research
*Reflecting a deep study of the UK Welfare Ecosystem.*

Having conducted an in-depth analysis of the UK benefits landscape over the last 3 years, this portfolio reflects an understanding of the specific tensions between statutory policy and technical delivery:

* **Universal Credit & Legacy Benefits:** Understanding the complexity of the "Managed Migration" from six legacy benefits to a single unified payment.
* **The "Swivel Chair" Problem:** Addressing the operational inefficiency where Case Managers must manually copy data between JSaps/Legacy systems and new digital front-ends.
* **GDS Service Standard:** Designing requirements that adhere to Government Digital Service principles—prioritising user needs, accessibility, and iterative delivery.
* **Policy Thresholds:** Application of specific business rules (e.g., the £16,000 capital limit) into logical acceptance criteria.
